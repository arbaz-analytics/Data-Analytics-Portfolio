# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## 📌 Business Problem

Analyze e-commerce sales data to evaluate business performance across regions, product categories, customers, and time periods. The objective is to identify revenue drivers, detect underperforming areas, and provide data-driven insights to support business decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Source:** Internal Sales Records
- **Total Records:** 88 Orders
- **Dataset Period:** May 2026 – August 2026
- **Data Snapshot:** August 2026

---

## 🛠️ Approach

- Cleaned and structured raw sales data into a standardized dataset.
- Built PivotTables to summarize sales performance by Region, Category, Month, and Customer.
- Developed KPI cards using Excel formulas to monitor key business metrics.
- Created interactive visualizations to communicate business performance effectively:
  - Regional Sales Analysis
  - Monthly Sales Trend
  - Category-wise Sales Distribution
  - Top 5 Customers by Sales

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Total Sales | **$3.47M** |
| Total Profit | **$514.5K** |
| Total Quantity Sold | **278** |
| Average Order Value | **$39.4K** |

---

## 📊 Key Business Findings

### 1. Regional Performance

- The **North** region generated the highest sales (**$1.37M**), while the **East** region recorded the lowest (**$0.29M**).
- This may indicate differences in regional business performance. Additional data such as marketing spend, delivery performance, and customer demand would be required to determine the root cause.

---

### 2. Category Performance

- **Electronics** contributed approximately **55%** of total sales, whereas **Office Supplies** contributed only **4%**.
- This indicates a heavy dependence on Electronics revenue and highlights an opportunity to improve performance in lower-contributing categories.

---

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

- Sales reached their highest level in **June 2026** before declining during **July** and **August**.
- A longer historical dataset would be required to determine whether this represents seasonality or a temporary business fluctuation.

---

### 4. Customer Contribution

- The **Top 5 customers** contributed a significant portion of overall revenue.
- Strengthening customer retention strategies could help reduce potential revenue concentration risk.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Investigate factors affecting lower-performing regions using operational and marketing data.
- Improve visibility and promotional strategies for Office Supplies.
- Analyze historical sales data over a longer period to validate seasonal trends.
- Develop customer retention initiatives for high-value customers.

- Added dashboard metadata including dataset period, total records, and data source for reporting transparency.
